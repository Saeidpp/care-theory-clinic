UNDERSTANDING THE TREATMENT

What is Profhilo?

Profhilo is an innovative injectable treatment containing a high concentration of hyaluronic acid. It is designed to improve skin hydration and support smoother, firmer and more radiant-looking skin.

Unlike traditional dermal fillers, Profhilo is not primarily designed to add volume or change facial contours. Instead, it works within the skin to improve overall skin quality, hydration and elasticity, helping to achieve a refreshed, naturally healthy-looking appearance.

It is particularly suited to those noticing early signs of skin ageing, loss of elasticity or dehydration.

After your appointment

Aftercare matters.

Small injection-site bumps, redness, tenderness, swelling or bruising can occur temporarily. The injection sites usually settle as the product disperses, although the timing varies.

Follow your practitioner's individual aftercare instructions. Contact the clinic if you have concerns. Seek urgent medical assessment for unusual or severe symptoms, particularly changes in skin colour, increasing pain or visual symptoms.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Is Profhilo a dermal filler?

No. Profhilo is an injectable hyaluronic acid treatment primarily intended to improve skin hydration and quality, rather than create conventional filler volume or contour.

How many sessions will I need?

The recommended treatment course depends on your skin, the product protocol and your practitioner's assessment. This will be discussed during your consultation.

When will I notice the results?

Results develop over time and vary between individuals. Your practitioner can discuss realistic expectations and follow-up timing.

Is there any downtime?

Most people can return to normal activities relatively quickly, but temporary injection-site bumps, swelling, bruising and tenderness may occur.

Is Profhilo suitable for everyone?

No injectable treatment is suitable for everyone. A medical history and suitability assessment are essential before proceeding.
