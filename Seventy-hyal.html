UNDERSTANDING THE TREATMENT

What is Profhilo Structura?

Profhilo Structura is a hyaluronic acid injectable designed to support facial tissue and address changes in facial volume associated with ageing. It is intended to help restore a naturally refreshed appearance.

Profhilo Structura differs from traditional dermal fillers. It is designed to support facial tissue and address changes in facial volume, rather than simply adding volume to individual lines or contours. Treatment is tailored to each person's facial structure and aesthetic goals.

It is particularly suited to those noticing early signs of skin ageing, loss of elasticity or dehydration.

After your appointment

Aftercare matters.

Small injection-site bumps, redness, tenderness, swelling or bruising can occur temporarily. The injection sites usually settle as the product disperses, although the timing varies.

Follow your practitioner's individual aftercare instructions. Contact the clinic if you have concerns. Seek urgent medical assessment for unusual or severe symptoms, particularly changes in skin colour, increasing pain or visual symptoms.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

What is Profhilo Structura?

Profhilo Structura is an injectable hyaluronic acid treatment designed to support facial tissue and address age-related changes in facial volume.

How is it different from regular Profhilo?

Profhilo is primarily used to improve skin hydration and quality, while Structura focuses more on supporting facial tissue and volume-related concerns.

How many treatments will I need?

Your practitioner will recommend a personalised treatment plan following a consultation. The number and timing of treatments depend on your individual needs.

When will I see results?

Results develop gradually and vary between individuals. Your practitioner will discuss expected outcomes during your consultation.

Is there any downtime?

Temporary redness, swelling, tenderness or bruising may occur at the injection sites. Recovery varies between individuals.

Who is suitable for Profhilo Structura?

It may be suitable for adults concerned about changes in facial tissue or volume. A consultation is required to assess suitability.

How much does it cost?

One session is £250, or two sessions are £450.
